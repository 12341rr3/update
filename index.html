Who We Are

Navisthaa Solutions is the Surface Augmentation specialist working with OEMs and Tier 1 Automotive Companies focused on elevating vehicle identity and perceived quality. We provide customised design and manufacturing expertise for integrated accessories—from Mood Lights and Interactive Surfaces to Soft Emblems and Sustainable merchandising —ensuring compliance, durability, and seamless integration into premium vehicle fleets.

Our Vision

Our Services

Explore our premium range of vehicle accessories designed for performance, style, and innovation.

Design Excellence

We design with purpose. Every accessory is engineered to solve a real user problem while maintaining perfect compatibility with OEM vehicle platforms. Our design approach blends precision, durability, and simplicity—resulting in products that look refined and perform flawlessly in real-world conditions.

New Product Development

Our NPD framework moves products from concept to validation with speed and clarity. Each stage—design, engineering, prototyping, testing, compliance—is tightly controlled to ensure predictability and market readiness.

Mass Production Capability

With our consortium partners, our production ecosystem is designed for scale, consistency, and efficiency. From raw material sourcing to final packaging, we use controlled processes that match OEM expectations. Lean manufacturing and real-time quality monitoring ensure reliability at every stage.
